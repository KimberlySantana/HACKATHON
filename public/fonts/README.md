Noto Sans TC Traditional Chinese font

The settlement PDF embeds a subset of this font for English, Traditional
Chinese, and mixed-language records. The bundled font was sourced from the
Traditional Chinese subset in the Fontsource distribution of Google Fonts'
Noto Sans TC and converted from WOFF to TrueType for PDF embedding. It is
distributed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1; see OFL.txt.
